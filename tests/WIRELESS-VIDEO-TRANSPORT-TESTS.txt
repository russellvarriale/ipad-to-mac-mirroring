Wireless video transport focused regression checks
=================================================
Run from the project root:

  python3 tests/run_video_transport_tests.py

The runner compiles the current WirelessVideoTransport.swift with the test
harness and a tiny local C subprocess fixture. A temporary directory holds
all generated sources, compiler caches, and binaries and is removed on exit.
It does not launch the app, listen on a network, access an iPad, or record video.

Result: 94 checks passed.

Coverage includes every packet split, one-byte fragmentation, exact 16 MiB
payload acceptance, invalid magic/kind/reserved/length rejection, oversized
length rejection before allocation, truncated EOF, synchronous consumer
rejection, reset-flood work limits, real socket delivery and EOF, expected
Stop suppression, and Process.standardInput duplication to child fd 0.

These focused framing and IPC checks do not establish end-to-end AirPlay,
video-decoder, UI, or recording behavior.
